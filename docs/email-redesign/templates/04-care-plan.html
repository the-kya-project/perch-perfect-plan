The care plan

Writing down what normal looks like

The care plan is where you keep what you know about your bird's care. It has six sections: Food, Routine, Behavior, Home, Health, and Emergency.

Six sections. The two in green are the easiest place to begin.

You don't have to fill in every section right away. Food and Routine are useful places to start, since those are things you'd need to explain to someone else looking after your bird.

Make the details useful

Write it as if the person reading it hasn't cared for your bird before. What do you offer at breakfast, and how much? When do they settle down for the night? What does someone need to know to follow that routine?

In Behavior, include what your bird enjoys and how they spend their day. Enrichment belongs in the care plan too.

The plan is as much for you as for anyone else. It gives you one place to check the details and update them when their care changes.

It's also what you share when someone else looks after your bird, which I'll cover in a few days.
