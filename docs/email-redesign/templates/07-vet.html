Having those details in front of you helps you explain what you've noticed. It gives your avian vet more context alongside what they learn from examining your bird.

The vet summary lets you review the information available in your bird's record before a visit. Check that it's up to date, and make a note of anything you want to ask.

Bring whatever you've recorded so far. You don't need a complete record to ask for help, and you shouldn't delay an appointment to collect more entries.
