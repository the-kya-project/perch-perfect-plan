Journal and moments

The bits worth remembering

The journal is for the things you want to remember about living with your bird: the first time they said something new, a photo from a good afternoon, the week they decided they hated the vacuum cleaner.

ExampleSaturday, 14 September

Tried the new foraging box this morning. Worked it out in under a minute, then ignored it for the rest of the day.

Moment · 3 October, the day Juno came home

An entry can be short. A sentence about what happened that day is enough.

Moments work a little differently. They're the dates you want to hold on to, like a hatch day or the day your bird came home.

You can keep everyday observations in the journal too. How they got on with a new foraging activity, or something you want to mention at their next vet appointment. Having the date and details written down can help later.

There's no schedule to keep up with here. Add something when there's something you want to remember.
