Sharing access

Sharing your bird's care

There are two ways to let someone else help with your bird's care in Kya & Co. Which one you use depends on how they're helping.

Sitter link

A sitter link is for someone looking after your bird during a sit. They can refer to the care plan while you're away.

No account needed. They open a private link.

Household

Household access is for people who help regularly. It's the option for sharing the ongoing work of keeping your bird's record up to date.

They sign in with their own account.

Before sharing the plan, check that feeding instructions and contact details are current. Talk through anything you'd want to show the person in practice, too.

The plan gives them somewhere to check the details afterward, so they don't have to remember everything you told them at once.
