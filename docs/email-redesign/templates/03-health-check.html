A daily habit

The daily health check

The daily check is a short set of questions about how your bird looks and behaves. It asks about things like alertness, appetite, droppings, breathing, and posture.

Working through the same questions helps you pay attention to those details. It also leaves you a record to look back on when you're trying to remember when something changed.

Worth knowing

The normal days belong in the record too. If your bird seems quieter than usual, it helps to be able to look back at what you've been seeing over the past few days.

The check can't confirm that your bird is healthy. If something worries you, contact your avian vet, even if the app hasn't flagged anything.
