How to weigh a bird with opinions

Grams count!

Weight is one of the most useful things you can track. It can shift before anything else looks different, and a record of it helps you and your vet get to know what's usual for your bird.

What you'll need

1
A digital scale that reads in grams. Make sure it can handle your bird plus any perch or container.
2
Somewhere steady to stand. Set the perch or container on the scale, press tare to zero it, then add your bird. Bird scales with a built-in perch make this easy.
3
A little patience. If the scale is new, let your bird check it out first. A treat helps. There's no need to force a first reading.

Weigh at about the same time each day, at the same point in their feeding routine. That's what makes one reading easy to compare with the next. And follow any instructions your vet has given you.
