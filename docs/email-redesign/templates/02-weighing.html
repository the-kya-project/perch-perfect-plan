Getting started

Weighing your bird

Keeping track of your bird's weight helps you get to know what's usual for them. Weight loss can be an early sign that something has changed, even when your bird looks much the same.

What you need

●A digital scale that reads in grams, with enough capacity for your bird and any perch or container you use.
●A stable place for your bird to stand. If you use a perch or container, put it on the scale first and press tare to reset the number to zero. Then add your bird. You can also get a scale specially made for weighing birds, with a perch built in.

Try to weigh at roughly the same time and at the same point in their usual feeding routine. That makes the readings easier to compare. Follow any instructions your vet has given you.

If the scale is new to your bird, give them time to get comfortable with it. You don't need to force a first reading.
