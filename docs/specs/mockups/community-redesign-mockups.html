Feed

Final-01-Feed

  • Bottom bar: Feed, Journeys, gold center plus (Add something), Library, My birds (back to the care app).
  • Header: large menu button beside the Kya & Co. logo, Updates bell on the right, title "Feed" (or the selected space name). No Spaces button, avatar, or pill row.
  • Journeys: story circles. First is "Your journey" with a gold plus; rings mark something new. Tap opens that journey’s updates.
  • "Try today" daily idea card (only when daily idea data exists).
  • "From the spaces": compact cards, body clamped to 3 lines, photo as a 92px square thumbnail on the right with a +N badge, space tag, Great job and Comment, and a ··· menu for report and block.

Side menu

Final-02-Menu

  • Drawer from the left, full screen height, dimmed backdrop, close button.
  • Order: Community guidelines (outlined row), Feed, SPACES box (Start here, Learn together, Do good, each with a dot, one-line description, and unread badge), tiles for Journeys (badge = new updates from people I follow), my chapter by name (badge = new chapter activity), and Events (no badge), then MY BIRDS (Home, Sits), then the account section (avatar, name, "Your profile and posts", Notifications and Settings buttons).
  • Badges hide at zero. Badge numbers shown are samples.

Community guidelines

The Flock is a place to get better at caring for our birds, together. These guidelines keep it kind, useful, and safe for everyone.

Every bird deserves a good life, and most mistakes come from not knowing, not from not caring.

We share what we’ve learned so the next bird, and the next person, has an easier time.

Kindness first, no shaming, and assume good intent.
We share experiences, but we never diagnose.
What belongs where, and how Do good posts are reviewed.
How the Kya team handles reports and removals.
