Welcome to the Flock

Before you join, here's a quick look at what we're all about.

Bridging research and life with birds

Researchers
Conservationists
Parrot owners

The Flock brings these communities together. Our care recommendations come from avian research and veterinary science, and our mission is a place where all of us collaborate.

What we advocate for

A varied, fresh diet

We advocate for fresh foods and a quality pellet.

Fully flighted birds

Research shows how beneficial flight is for birds, so we advocate for keeping them flighted whenever possible.

Good husbandry

That means an appropriately sized cage, daily enrichment, plenty of foraging, and time out of the cage.

Positive reinforcement training

Research shows it's the best way to train and bond with a bird. We don't advocate for dominance or punishment-based methods.

Most importantly, we're building a bigger table

We separate the issue from the person. We're all still learning, so wherever you and your bird are today, you're welcome here.
