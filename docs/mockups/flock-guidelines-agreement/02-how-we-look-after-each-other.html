How we look after each other

This is what makes the Flock different.

We give feedback only when it's asked for If someone asks, "What do you think of my setup?", share kind, honest ideas. If someone just shares a photo of their bird, celebrate the bird, and please don't pile on in the comments.
We flag it instead of calling it out If something someone shares worries you, tap flag. The Kya team reviews every single one and reaches out privately, so nobody gets corrected in front of everyone.
Health questions go to your vet We never give medical advice here. You're welcome to share what's worked for you, but none of us are avian vets, and posts that give medical advice will be taken down.
What you share stays here We never feature your posts or photos anywhere else without asking you first.
